# Economics

IDART treats economics as a separate product choice from market behavior.

## Economics Modes

### Creator First
Prioritizes creator incentives while retaining a platform share.

### All-Win
Designed to balance incentives among creator, holder-reward allocation, and IDART.

### Holder Rewards
Designed to allocate a larger portion toward future holder participation while preserving creator and platform incentives.

## Implementation Note
Meteora DBC natively provides creator/partner fee sharing.

Holder rewards require an additional IDART rewards layer. Until that module is fully deployed, holder-reward percentages are treated as an intended/earmarked allocation rather than represented as automatic on-chain distribution.
