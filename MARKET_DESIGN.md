# Market Design

IDART separates market design into simple creator-facing decisions.

## Quote Asset
Creators can choose between traditional Solana quote assets and stock-related assets supported by the product.

## Market Profile
A Market Profile answers: **How should this market launch and behave?**

Current product concepts:
- Equity Quote
- Discovery
- Balanced

## Graduation
IDART is designed around a DBC-to-DAMM-v2 lifecycle.

## Market DNA
Each market is designed to expose:
- quote asset
- market profile
- economics mode
- trading-fee model
- creator/platform allocation
- graduation destination
- migration state
